Connecting the AirLynx Gateway Components

   1. Set-up the AirLynx Wireless Gateway. Plug an Ethernet cable from the Ethernet
      Switch to the PoE Injector “Lan” Connector and from PoE Injector “PoE”
      Connector to Access Point “PoE” Connector. Plug in the PoE Injector Power
      Supply. Attach one antenna. (not pictured)

   2. Connect the Access Point Pigtail (G) from the open antenna port on the access
      point to the DC Injector (C) connector labeled “To AP”.

   3. Connect one end of the Antenna Cable (E) to the Amplifier (D) connector
      labeled “To Antenna”

   4. Connect the other end of the Antenna Cable (E) to the Antenna (B)

   5. Plug in the Power Supply (H) to the DC Injector (C)

   6. The access point is configured with the IP address 192.168.0.3x with SSID =
      “Lynx” as labeled.
