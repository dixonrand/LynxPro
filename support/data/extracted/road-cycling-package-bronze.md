                                                              Bronze Package Benefits
                 The technology included in the Bronze Package offers clear advantages over other
                 timing systems at similar price points. The EtherLynx Vision photo-finish camera
                 captures 2,000 images every second. Compare that to a video camera system which
                 maxes out at just 120 fps. FinishLynx ensures that close finishes don’t get lost
                 between the missing frames. Lynx cameras also offer clear advantages over RFID
                 chips and transponders. While some transponders may claim accuracy to 1/100th of
                 a second or more, they provide no visual verification for close finishes and may
                 provide incorrect finish orders at faster speeds. FinishLynx provides faster and more
                 accurate results in real time. Plus you don’t have to worry about losing, breaking, or
                 collecting expensive transponders after every race.
