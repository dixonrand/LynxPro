VDM (Video Display Module)
VDM enables the live display of FinishLynx running time, results, and images on a video display
connected to the HDMI port of Vision cameras. Simply connect a compatible video display to the camera
back and instantly show live results data at the finish line.

ResulTV
ResulTV is a digital display and graphic generation program that produces live results from FinishLynx
timing systems, and can also be used with LynxPad and FieldLynx. The Windows-based software allows
FinishLynx operators to produce scalable result graphics for output on any compatible graphic display or
scoreboard. The ResulTV software can even produce high-quality dynamic images suitable for live
broadcast television or internet video streams.

content of the ResulTV-VDM layout is controlled by the data received using customizable scripts.
